# Audio Environment Fingerprinter Wiki

A cross-platform desktop research beta for reproducible, explainable acoustic-environment screening. It hashes the original file
and separately computes a versioned acoustic feature fingerprint.

It does not identify a physical location, estimate the probability of a common location, or establish authenticity. Its output
requires examiner review, and the change-point threshold and similarity score are not validated on representative forensic data
(README).
